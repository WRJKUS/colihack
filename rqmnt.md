Judged on 5 things
Each criterion is scored **1–5** and multiplied by its weight, so the maximum score is **75**. Weights reflect the vision: substance over showmanship.

#### 1 Automation depth ×5 points
"Does the accountant just sign?"
How much of the accounting loop runs end-to-end with no human in the middle — captured, processed, checked, enriched, categorised and booked automatically. The closer to one-click sign-off, the higher the score.
Example: invoice → PEPPOL → double-entry, hands-off.

#### 2 Trust & compliance ×4 points
"Safe to sign."
Is the output correct, auditable and defensible? Handles VAT/tax right, respects privacy and data governance, and shows its sources — so an accountant can sign without fear and a business avoids fines.
Example: PEPPOL-compliant, valid, auditable.

#### 3 Real-world pull ×3 points
"An SME would pay for this."
Solves a genuine pain, WhatsApp-simple to adopt, and fiscally interesting — the "€50-a-year accountant who just signs" dream. Clear user, clear value. Would a real Belgian SME use it on Monday?
Example: a business actually sends & books an invoice
#### 4 Agentic execution x2 points
"It actually works."
Quality of the build and the agentic design: agents given real authority or a real budget, data structured so automation is obvious, and a working demo that lands in 5 minutes.
Example: agents that invoice with real authority.

#### 5 Compelling pitch ×1 point
"5 minutes that land."
Tells a sharp story in 5 minutes: the problem, the demo, why it matters. Clear, confident and memorable — the judges should leave knowing exactly what you built and why it's a step toward automatic accounting.
Example: clarity, narrative and a demo that convinces.
